Programmes & Initiatives

Programmes built for the nation

From citizen subsidies to industrial decarbonisation, our programmes address every dimension of the energy transition.

01
☀️

Residential Solar Subsidy Scheme

Grants covering up to 40% of rooftop solar installation costs for qualifying households, paired with low-interest government finance.

02
🏭

Industrial Decarbonisation Fund

€4.2 billion ring-fenced for heavy industry retrofits: hydrogen-ready boilers, electrification of heat, and carbon capture pilot projects.

03
🔋

Community Battery Storage

Grid-scale battery installations in 200 communities nationwide, stabilising local supply and reducing peak import costs by an estimated 22%.

04
🌬️

Offshore Wind Expansion

Licensing and co-investment in 18 GW of new offshore wind capacity by 2030, supporting 85,000 new green jobs in coastal regions.

05
🏘️

Fuel Poverty Alleviation Grant

Targeted support for low-income households: insulation upgrades, heat pump installations, and energy literacy workshops at no cost.

06
⚛️

Next-Generation Nuclear Research

Funding for small modular reactor (SMR) technology development and site assessment at the forefront of 4th-generation nuclear.

On track for our 2030 net-zero target

Since 2015, we have reduced coal generation from 31% to 0%, accelerated wind and solar deployment, and invested in grid flexibility infrastructure that supports intermittent renewables at scale.

Our 2026–2030 National Energy Plan sets out the pathway to eliminate fossil fuel generation from the grid entirely, backed by a €28 billion public investment programme.

Operations Specialist – Customer Platforms

Customer Experience · Remote / Hybrid · Full-Time

About the Role

Nexus Energy Authority is looking for a talented Operations Specialist to join our growing Customer Experience team. You will own the day-to-day management of our customer platform stack, drive data quality initiatives, and work cross-functionally with Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success to optimise the full customer lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and maintain our customer engagement platform, ensuring data integrity and system health
  • Design, build, and optimise automated workflows, sequences, and lifecycle campaigns
  • Partner with Sales & Marketing to segment audiences and execute targeted outreach programmes
  • Create dashboards and reports that surface actionable insights to senior stakeholders
  • Identify gaps in operational processes and lead continuous improvement initiatives
  • Conduct platform training sessions and produce documentation for internal teams
  • Ensure compliance with GDPR and data governance policies across all customer data activities
  • Evaluate and integrate third-party tools and data sources to enrich our platform ecosystem

What We're Looking For

  • 3+ years of hands-on experience with customer engagement or operations platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot, or similar preferred)
  • Strong understanding of B2C and B2B sales pipelines and customer lifecycle management
  • Proficiency in automation tools, email marketing platforms, and basic SQL or data tools
  • Analytical mindset — comfortable working with large datasets and translating data into clear insights
  • Excellent communication skills in English; additional European languages are a strong plus
  • Familiarity with GDPR compliance requirements and best practices for data management

Nice to Have

  • Platform certifications (Salesforce Administrator, HubSpot, or equivalent)
  • Experience with CDP platforms (e.g. Segment, Tealium) or marketing automation suites
  • Background in subscription-based or energy/utilities businesses

What Nexus Offers

  • Remote-first culture with flexible working
  • €1,500/yr learning budget
  • Private health insurance
  • 30 days paid leave
  • Equity & performance bonus
  • Home office stipend
  • Dedicated career mentor
